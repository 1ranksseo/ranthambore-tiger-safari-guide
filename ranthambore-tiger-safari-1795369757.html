Conservation and Ecology: The Bengal Tiger Revival in Sawai Madhopur

Project Tiger transformed Ranthambore from a former royal hunting reserve into one of global conservation's greatest success stories. Securing an official ranthambore safari booking directly supports anti-poaching patrols and village eco-development initiatives.

Strict regulatory guidelines ensure vehicles remain on designated forest tracks, observe minimum distances from wildlife, and exit before sunset to preserve natural nocturnal predator behaviors.

Tourism revenue provides essential sustainable funding for local forest guards, community education programs, and continuous radio-collar monitoring of vulnerable big cat lineages.
